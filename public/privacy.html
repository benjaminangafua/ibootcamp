What this website does

This website explains the proposed iBootCamp program and offers contact links. It does not collect application forms, student records, payment information, account registrations, or newsletter subscriptions. It does not use advertising trackers or website analytics in its current implementation. Fonts and images are hosted with the website.

Contact links

Clicking a WhatsApp link opens an external service with a suggested message. You choose whether to send it. WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy practices apply. A telephone link opens your device’s calling application. Please avoid sharing sensitive learner information, identity documents, or safeguarding details in an initial general inquiry.

Children and families

Learners under 18 should involve a parent or guardian when contacting the organization. This site is not an enrollment system. Any later enrollment process must provide its own consent, safeguarding, and information-handling arrangements.

Hosting and technical records

The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, request times, and security logs when serving this website. Those records are subject to the provider’s own service and privacy arrangements.

Program status and illustrations

iBootCamp is presented as a proposed nonprofit technology learning initiative. The site does not claim completed registration, external accreditation, secured funding, a confirmed venue, open admissions, or achieved impact. All targets and budget figures are planning assumptions. Classroom images are illustrative assets from the supplied program presentation, not evidence of enrolled participants or delivered sessions.
